Why translation matters

A global technology publication should help readers understand useful information in the language they are most comfortable using. The language selector should work on current articles and content added in the future, not only on a fixed list of existing pages.

Technology and cybersecurity

Strong cybersecurity depends on practical habits: use multi-factor authentication, install security updates, verify unexpected requests, and report suspicious activity promptly. A translation can make these steps easier to understand, but important security instructions should still be reviewed by a fluent speaker.

Business learning

Before automating a business process, document the current workflow, measure a baseline, identify the problem, and test one controlled change. Compare results using the same definitions and time period, and keep a rollback plan in case the change causes unexpected problems.

Prototype note: free public translation servers may be slow, unavailable, rate-limited, or inaccurate. Do not use this prototype as proof that every language is supported or that translations are publication-ready.
