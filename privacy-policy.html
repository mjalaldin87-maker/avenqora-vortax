Our approach to privacy

Avenqora Vortax aims to collect and use information responsibly and only for legitimate purposes connected with operating, improving and communicating about the website and its services.

1. Information We May Receive

When visitors contact Avenqora Vortax by email, information voluntarily included in the message may be received. This can include a name, email address, message details and any other information the sender chooses to provide.

2. Website Information

Basic technical information may be processed by hosting, security, analytics or other website infrastructure services where such services are used. The type of information available depends on the services connected to the website.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, manage editorial communication, maintain website security, improve website functionality and operate the Avenqora Vortax platform.

4. Email Communication

If you contact Avenqora Vortax by email, the information contained in your message may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain communication records or handle related business or editorial matters.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Avenqora Vortax may use cookies or similar technologies if required by website functionality, analytics, security or third-party services. The exact use of these technologies may change as the website develops.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, security, email, embedded content or other technical functions. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

7. Information Sharing

Avenqora Vortax does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide a requested service, or when required by applicable law.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information. However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. External Links

Avenqora Vortax may link to external websites and resources. Avenqora Vortax is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of those websites separately.

10. Children's Privacy

The Student Hub is intended as an educational resource for students, but the website is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Visitors should avoid submitting personal information that is not necessary for their inquiry.

11. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the information involved, individuals may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information.

12. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website, services and applicable requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
