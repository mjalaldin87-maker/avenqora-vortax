# Student Learning Roadmap

A flexible roadmap for Pakistani ICS students, university students, and international learners. Move through the stages at your own pace; the goal is evidence of learning, not simply collecting certificates.

## Stage 1 — Foundations
- [ ] Computer and operating-system basics
- [ ] Files, folders, browsers, and safe account practices
- [ ] Basic networking concepts
- [ ] HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript
- [ ] Basic command-line skills
- [ ] Learn how to read technical documentation

**Output:** one small web page and a short written explanation of how it works.

## Stage 2 — Choose a direction
Pick one starting path:
- Technology / web development
- Cybersecurity
- Business and analytics
- Digital skills / content and productivity

Do not try to master every path simultaneously.

## Stage 3 — Build projects
Create 2–4 small projects that are legal, safe, and explainable. Examples:
- A responsive portfolio page
- A simple data-analysis exercise
- A documented security-awareness checklist
- A small automation or productivity workflow

For every project record: problem, approach, tools, result, limitations, and what you learned.

## Stage 4 — Communication
- [ ] Write clear project descriptions.
- [ ] Learn basic professional email etiquette.
- [ ] Practice explaining technical ideas to a non-technical person.
- [ ] Keep a simple CV and portfolio updated.

## Stage 5 — Real-world practice
Look for supervised projects, internships, open-source contribution, freelance work appropriate to your skill level, or student competitions. Never claim professional experience you do not have.

## Stage 6 — Specialize
After learning the fundamentals, choose a narrower area. Examples include frontend development, backend development, cloud, security operations, application security, data analytics, or digital marketing.

## Weekly study loop
1. Learn one concept.
2. Practice it.
3. Build something small.
4. Write down what failed.
5. Review authoritative documentation.
6. Publish or save a clean explanation of the result.

### Important
For cybersecurity practice, use only systems and environments you are authorized to test. This roadmap is educational guidance, not a guarantee of employment or certification.
