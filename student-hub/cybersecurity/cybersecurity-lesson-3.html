What is authentication?

Authentication is the process of checking whether someone is really the person they claim to be. A password is one common authentication method, but passwords can be stolen, guessed or reused.

What does multi-factor authentication mean?

MFA uses more than one type of authentication factor. The factors are commonly grouped into something you know, something you have and something you are.

Three common factor types

Know: a password or PIN.

Have: a phone, security key or authentication device.

Are: a biometric characteristic such as a fingerprint.

Why does MFA help?

If a password is stolen, an attacker may still need the second factor. This creates another barrier between the attacker and the account.

Examples of MFA

  • Authenticator app code
  • Security key
  • Passkey
  • Push approval
  • Biometric verification used with another factor

What should you do with an unexpected MFA request?

Do not approve a login that you did not start. An unexpected request may indicate that someone has your password or is attempting to access your account. Verify the activity through a trusted method and report suspicious requests when appropriate.

How students can use MFA safely

  1. Enable MFA on important accounts.
  2. Prefer stronger phishing-resistant options when available.
  3. Keep recovery methods protected.
  4. Never share verification codes with another person.
  5. Review account sign-in alerts.

Quick review

MFA adds another layer of account protection by requiring more than one authentication factor. It does not make an account impossible to compromise, but it can reduce the damage caused by stolen passwords when implemented correctly.
